# Draft email to PD Guide

**To:** hello@pdguide.org (or via https://www.pdguide.org/contact)
**Subject:** Permission to surface PD Guide events on our internal faculty calendar

---

Hi PD Guide team,

I'm the Senior Director of Academic Systems at Grace Church School, an
independent JK–12 school in New York City. We've built a small, internal
professional-development platform for our faculty and staff, and PD Guide
has turned out to be the best-curated source of events for independent
schools that we've found — thank you for maintaining it.

I'd like to check in before we lean on it. We'd like to surface a
relevant subset of your listings on our internal PD calendar, and we've
built it to respect your work:

- We read only the **factual event details** (title, dates, format,
  location, cost) and link each event to the **host organization's own
  page** — never to PD Guide, and never through an affiliate link.
- We **don't** reproduce your directory, your arrangement, or your
  topic/role tagging; events map to our own internal categories.
- It's **internal only** — visible to our ~200 employees behind a login,
  never republished publicly and never a competing directory.
- An admin reviews and approves each event before it's shown, and we
  verify details against the host before anything reaches a calendar.
- Technically it's a **light, once-weekly read** of your events page.

Your terms permit use "for your own or your school's use," so I believe
this is well within bounds — but given it's a standing part of our
system, I'd rather ask than assume. Are you comfortable with this?

And if you'd prefer we consume the data a particular way — an existing
feed, an attribution line, or anything else — just let me know and we'll
set it up accordingly. Happy to share more about how we use it.

With appreciation for the resource,

Ravendra Persaud
Senior Director of Academic Systems
Grace Church School
rpersaud@gcschool.org
